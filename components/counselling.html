Counselling Ministry

At Charis Missionary Church Pretoria, we believe that healing flows not only through prayer and worship but also through listening hearts and compassionate guidance. Life's challenges can feel overwhelming, but you do not have to walk through them alone. Our counselling ministry is here to provide a safe, confidential and Spirit-led space where you can find encouragement, restoration and clarity.

Whether you are facing personal struggles, family challenges or seeking direction in your spiritual journey, Apostle R Khorommbi is committed to walking alongside you with love, wisdom and biblical truth. We trust the Holy Spirit to bring comfort and transformation as we journey together.

If you are in need of prayer, guidance or simply someone to listen, we warmly invite you to connect with Apostle R Khorommbi who is available to walk with you in faith, offering biblical counsel and Spirit-led encouragement.

Together we will seek God's wisdom and healing for your life.
